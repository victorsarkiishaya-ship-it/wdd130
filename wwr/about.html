Splash Rafting Company

Our purpose is to provide thrilling, safe, and unforgettable white water rafting adventures for everyone. Our mission is to connect people with the power and beauty of the river through expert guides, quality equipment, and a passion for the outdoors. Come live the adventure!

History

Splash Rafting Company was founded in 2015 by a group of friends who shared one dream: to share the joy of the river with the world. What began as a single raft and a lot of enthusiasm has grown into a leading adventure company with experienced guides, modern safety equipment, and thousands of happy adventurers every season.
